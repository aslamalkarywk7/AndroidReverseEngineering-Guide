# Introduction

Welcome to the **Android Reverse Engineering Handbook**.  
This book is a comprehensive educational resource (target: ~450 pages) that teaches **reverse engineering of Android applications** using open-source tools.  

## Scope and Purpose
- To provide **practical, step-by-step guidance** for analyzing Android apps.  
- To focus on **lawful, ethical use cases**: auditing your own apps, academic learning, and authorized security testing.  
- To cover both **static analysis** (examining binaries and resources without execution) and **dynamic analysis** (instrumenting apps while running).  

## Why Android Reverse Engineering?
- **Security auditing**: discover vulnerabilities, insecure data handling, and weak crypto.  
- **Learning internals**: understand Dalvik/ART, Smali bytecode, and JNI bridges.  
- **Debugging & research**: see how protections like obfuscation and packing work.  
- **Innovation**: learn how to build stronger, more secure apps.  

## What This Handbook Covers
- Android OS architecture and APK internals.  
- Tools: APKTool, JADX, Ghidra, Frida, Objection, Androguard, MobSF, Cutter/Rizin, etc.  
- Techniques: unpacking APKs, decoding resources, Smali patching, SSL pinning bypass.  
- Case studies: applying these tools in controlled lab exercises.  
- Appendices: cheat sheets, quick references, and scripts.  

> **Legal Reminder**: Only reverse engineer apps you own or are explicitly authorized to test. Unauthorized analysis of proprietary apps may be illegal.
